Murmur is a concept for a small AI brand with a simple belief: helpful technology should make space for people, not get in their way.

This one-page website explores that vision. Murmur AI is a fictional placeholder name, not an available AI product. The examples are prewritten; there's no chatbot, signup, or payment system here.
