Small by design

Not the loudest in the room.
Just a different kind of idea.

Murmur is a concept for a small AI brand with a simple belief: helpful technology should make space for people, not get in their way.

This one-page website explores that vision. Murmur AI is a fictional placeholder name, not an available AI product. The samples and chat replies are prewritten; there is no live AI model, signup, or payment system here.
